// 01 — About

The Analyst
Behind the Terminal.

I'm Qais Eyad, an Information Security Engineer based in Amman, Jordan, with deep hands-on experience in Security Operations Center (SOC) analysis, Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR), and Blue Team operations.

My work centers on one mission: detect threats before they escalate. I analyze logs across network, endpoint, and server layers, hunt for Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), and respond to incidents using SIEM platforms like QRadar and Splunk, SOAR automation, and EDR tooling.

Beyond the SOC, I'm passionate about knowledge transfer. I teach, mentor, and create content that empowers the next wave of cybersecurity professionals — through YouTube labs, university events, and live security sessions.

I hold a B.Sc. in Cyber Security from Hashemite University (2025), where I graduated with a Very Good GPA and led multiple award-winning projects in malware analysis and DFIR.
